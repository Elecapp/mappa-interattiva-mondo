About this Project

This site maps evidence of human mobility across late medieval Europe — pilgrims, merchants, students, diplomats, craftworkers, and people forced from their homes — through the objects and documents that record their journeys. Each point on the map is an item with a known or approximate place of origin, creation, or discovery.

How the map works

Every item on the map comes from a single spreadsheet maintained by the project team. Adding, editing, or removing an item only requires editing a row in that spreadsheet — the website updates automatically, with no need to touch any code.
